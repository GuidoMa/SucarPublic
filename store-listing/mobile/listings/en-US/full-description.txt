Sucar! shows your glucose readings on your car's display through Android Auto, so you can keep an eye on your levels without looking at your phone while driving.

The app connects to a Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) data service you already use:
- LibreView / LibreLinkUp
- Dexcom Share
- Nightscout
- xDrip+ or Juggluco, directly from the same phone, if you use one of those apps

Your current reading and a short history appear only in Android Auto, live and hands-free. The phone screen itself is for setup: choose your data source, enter your credentials, and adjust display preferences there.

If you use xDrip+ or Juggluco, point them at the app package com.gma.sucar.mobile.

Sucar! has no ads, no analytics, and no crash-reporting services. It doesn't collect or share your data - readings go directly between your configured CGM service and your device.

Sucar! displays data from your existing CGM setup. It does not replace your CGM sensor, reader, or any medical device, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
