Sucar! is an experimental app for Android Automotive OS that runs standalone on your vehicle's built-in screen - no phone required.

It shows a live glucose chart alongside your settings, right on the head unit, connecting to a Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) data service you already use:
- LibreView / LibreLinkUp
- Dexcom Share
- Nightscout

Data comes from the cloud CGM service you configure; a phone or sensor uploads it there first, and Sucar! retrieves it directly on the car's screen.

This build does not use the Android Automotive app framework and is distributed to internal testers only, not the general public.

Sucar! has no ads, no analytics, and no crash-reporting services. It doesn't collect or share your data beyond fetching it from the CGM service you configure.

Sucar! displays data from your existing CGM setup. It does not replace your CGM sensor, reader, or any medical device, and it is not a substitute for professional medical advice.
