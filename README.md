# Sucar!

Sucar! is a glucose monitoring app that brings Continuous Glucose Monitor (CGM) data into the car.

It comes in two variants:

- **Android Auto** — runs on your phone and shows your glucose data on the car's display through Android Auto. The phone screen itself is settings-only.
- **Android Automotive OS** — an experimental, sideload-only variant that runs standalone on the vehicle's built-in screen. It is not available on the Play Store.

Both variants read glucose data from a service you already use — LibreView/LibreLinkUp, Dexcom Share, Nightscout, or, on the phone variant, directly from xDrip+ or Juggluco on the same device.
